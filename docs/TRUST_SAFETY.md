# Trust & Safety Guidelines

## Our Mission

Marcela Jacon Academy is committed to providing a safe, trustworthy environment where mentees can learn and grow with confidence. These guidelines outline how we ensure trust and safety across our platform.

## Core Trust Principles

### 1. Verified Mentors

Every mentor on our platform undergoes a comprehensive verification process:

#### Identity Verification
- Government-issued photo ID validation
- Facial recognition match
- Address verification

#### Credential Validation
- Educational degrees and certificates
- Professional certifications
- Work experience verification
- Reference checks from colleagues or supervisors

#### Professional Standards Review
- Review of work history
- Assessment of mentoring capabilities
- Background screening (optional, based on region and service type)

**Verification Badge**: Verified mentors display a badge on their profile, giving mentees confidence in their legitimacy.

### 2. Transparent Reviews

Our review system ensures authenticity and transparency:

#### Verified Reviews
- Only actual mentees who completed sessions can leave reviews
- Reviews are verified against session records
- No fake or incentivized reviews allowed

#### Comprehensive Feedback
- Overall rating (1-5 stars)
- Category ratings:
  - Communication
  - Expertise
  - Helpfulness
  - Professionalism
  - Value for time
- Written feedback
- Response from mentors (optional)

#### Review Integrity
- Reviews cannot be deleted (only hidden if violating guidelines)
- Mentors cannot pay to remove negative reviews
- Suspected fake reviews investigated and removed

### 3. Secure Communication

All communication is protected:

#### Platform-Only Communication
- Initial conversations must happen on the platform
- Built-in messaging system with encryption
- Video call integration with security features
- Screen sharing with privacy controls

#### Privacy Protection
- No requirement to share personal contact information
- Optional phone/email sharing only after trust is established
- Report functionality within every conversation

### 4. Safe Payment System

Financial transactions are secure and transparent:

#### Protected Payments
- PCI DSS compliant payment processing
- Multiple payment method support
- Secure storage of payment information
- No direct cash payments required

#### Refund Policy
- Clear refund guidelines
- Dispute resolution process
- Protection against fraudulent charges
- Transparent fee structure

## Safety Features

### For Mentees

#### Before a Session

1. **Review Mentor Profile**
   - Check verification status
   - Read reviews from other mentees
   - Verify credentials and expertise
   - Review response rate and activity

2. **Initial Contact**
   - Start with platform messaging
   - Ask questions about their approach
   - Discuss your goals and expectations
   - Confirm availability and pricing

3. **Red Flags to Watch**
   - Requests to move conversation off-platform immediately
   - Pressure to make quick decisions
   - Requests for personal information early
   - Promises that sound too good to be true
   - Inconsistent information

#### During a Session

1. **Professional Boundaries**
   - Sessions should focus on mentorship topics
   - Mentor should maintain professional behavior
   - No inappropriate personal questions
   - Respect your time and scheduled duration

2. **Recording Sessions**
   - Platform allows session recording (with consent)
   - Useful for review and safety
   - Stored securely with encryption

3. **Emergency Features**
   - End session button available at all times
   - Quick report function during sessions
   - Support team available for immediate assistance

#### After a Session

1. **Leave a Review**
   - Share your honest experience
   - Help other mentees make informed decisions
   - Provide constructive feedback

2. **Report Issues**
   - Report any violations immediately
   - Provide details and evidence
   - Our team will investigate within 24 hours

### For Mentors

#### Maintaining Trust

1. **Accurate Representation**
   - Keep credentials up to date
   - Honest about expertise areas
   - Clear about availability
   - Transparent about pricing

2. **Professional Conduct**
   - Maintain boundaries
   - Respect mentee confidentiality
   - Provide quality mentorship
   - Respond promptly to inquiries

3. **Protecting Yourself**
   - Keep all communication on platform initially
   - Document sessions appropriately
   - Report suspicious mentee behavior
   - Use platform's payment system

## Reporting System

### What to Report

Report any:
- Inappropriate behavior
- Harassment or bullying
- Fraudulent activity
- Impersonation
- Privacy violations
- Security concerns
- Spam or scams

### How to Report

1. **In-App Reporting**
   - Report button on profiles and messages
   - Quick report during sessions
   - Detailed report form available

2. **Email**
   - safety@marcelajacon.academy
   - Include relevant details and evidence

3. **Emergency**
   - For immediate safety concerns
   - Call local authorities if needed
   - Then report to us

### What Happens Next

1. **Immediate Response**
   - Report acknowledged within 24 hours
   - Urgent cases handled immediately
   - Temporary measures if needed (account suspension)

2. **Investigation**
   - Thorough review of evidence
   - Contact with involved parties
   - Protection of reporter's privacy

3. **Resolution**
   - Action taken based on findings
   - Notification to reporter
   - Appeals process available

4. **Follow-up**
   - Check-in with affected users
   - Platform improvements based on incidents
   - Transparency reports published

## Safety Policies

### Zero Tolerance

We have zero tolerance for:
- Sexual harassment or advances
- Discrimination or hate speech
- Threats or violence
- Fraud or scams
- Sharing private information without consent

**Consequence**: Immediate account termination and law enforcement notification if applicable.

### Graduated Response

For other violations:
1. **First Warning**: Educational guidance
2. **Second Warning**: Temporary suspension
3. **Third Warning**: Permanent ban

### Appeals Process

Users can appeal decisions:
- Submit appeal within 14 days
- Provide additional context or evidence
- Independent review by different team member
- Final decision within 7 days
