# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: February 12, 2026**

## Introduction

At Marcela Jacon Academy, we believe trust is built on transparency. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share your personal information when you use our mentorship platform.

## Our Privacy Principles

1. **Transparency**: We clearly explain what data we collect and why
2. **Minimal Collection**: We only collect data necessary for our services
3. **User Control**: You control your data and privacy settings
4. **Security**: We protect your data with industry-leading security measures
5. **No Selling**: We never sell your personal data to third parties

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

**Account Registration**
- Name and email address
- Username and password
- Profile photo (optional)
- Professional information (for mentors)
- Educational background (for mentees)

**Profile Information**
- Bio and interests
- Skills and expertise
- Career goals
- Availability and preferences

**Communication**
- Messages between mentors and mentees
- Reviews and ratings
- Support inquiries

**Payment Information**
- Payment card details (processed by third-party payment processors)
- Billing address
- Transaction history

**Verification Information (Mentors)**
- Government-issued ID
- Professional credentials
- Employment verification
- Background check results (optional)

### Information We Collect Automatically

**Usage Data**
- Pages visited and features used
- Time spent on the platform
- Click patterns and navigation
- Search queries

**Device Information**
- Device type and operating system
- Browser type and version
- IP address
- Language preferences

**Technical Data**
- Cookies and similar technologies
- Log files
- Error reports

## How We Use Your Information

### To Provide Our Services

- Create and maintain your account
- Match mentees with appropriate mentors
- Facilitate communication between users
- Process payments
- Provide customer support
- Send service-related notifications

### To Ensure Safety and Trust

- Verify mentor credentials
- Detect and prevent fraud
- Enforce our terms of service
- Investigate violations
- Comply with legal obligations

### To Improve Our Platform

- Analyze usage patterns
- Develop new features
- Optimize user experience
- Conduct research and analytics
- Test platform performance

### To Communicate With You

- Send platform updates
- Notify about new messages or reviews
- Share relevant opportunities
- Request feedback
- Marketing communications (with your consent)

## How We Share Your Information

### With Other Users

**Public Profile Information**
- Name and username
- Profile photo
- Bio and interests
- Reviews and ratings (for mentors)
- Availability status

**Private Information**
- Messages are only visible to sender and recipient
- Contact information shared only with matched mentors/mentees

### With Service Providers

We share data with trusted third-party providers who help us operate:

- Cloud hosting services
- Payment processors
- Email service providers
- Analytics platforms
- Verification services
- Customer support tools

All providers are contractually obligated to protect your data.

### For Legal Reasons

We may disclose information when required by law or to:

- Comply with legal processes
- Enforce our terms of service
- Protect rights, property, or safety
- Prevent fraud or security threats
- Respond to government requests

### With Your Consent

We will share your information with third parties only with your explicit consent.

## We Never

- Sell your personal data to third parties
- Share your private messages without consent
- Use your data for purposes not disclosed
- Share more data than necessary with partners

## Your Privacy Rights

### Access and Portability

- Request a copy of your data
- Download your information in machine-readable format
- Review all data we have about you

### Correction and Update

- Update your profile information
- Correct inaccurate data
- Complete incomplete information

### Deletion

- Delete your account and associated data
- Request removal of specific information
- Be forgotten (GDPR right to erasure)

### Control and Restriction

- Opt-out of marketing communications
- Disable certain data collection
- Restrict processing of your data
- Object to automated decision-making

### Privacy Settings

Manage your privacy through granular controls:

- Profile visibility (public, mentees/mentors only, private)
- Who can contact you
- Search visibility
- Activity status display
- Notification preferences

## Data Retention

- **Active Accounts**: Data retained while account is active
- **Deleted Accounts**: Most data deleted within 30 days
- **Legal Requirements**: Some data retained for compliance (e.g., transaction records)
- **Anonymized Data**: May retain anonymized data for analytics

## Security Measures

We protect your data using:

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Encryption at rest (AES-256)
- Regular security audits
- Access controls and authentication
- Employee training on data protection
- Incident response procedures

See our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) for more details.

## Children's Privacy

Our platform is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. For users aged 13-17, we require parental consent and provide additional protections.

## International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your residence. We ensure adequate protection through:

- Standard contractual clauses

- Appropriate safeguards
